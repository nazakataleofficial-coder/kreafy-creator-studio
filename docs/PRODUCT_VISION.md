# Product Vision

Kreafy Creator Studio enables YouTube and Facebook automation creators to produce professional content quickly from a topic, transcript or raw footage. One local-first application combines script writing, visual production, speech, clipping, timeline editing, captions, sound design and export. Standalone tools remain usable independently.

## Three main modes
1. Idea → complete faceless documentary/educational video.
2. Long footage → multiple quality Shorts/Reels.
3. Manual plus AI-assisted multitrack video editing.

## Differentiation
Free-first, hardware-aware, connected assets, reusable brand kits, project recovery, per-scene regeneration, explicit QA and transparent cloud spend. Do not claim unlimited generation or 100% defect-free AI visuals.
